SubLens

Live screen translation for Android · fully offline

Drag a transparent lens over YouTube subtitles, chats or any text on screen. The translation appears right on top of the original, and the reply line lets you answer in their language. On holiday, point the camera at a menu, a sign or the QR code on the table.

Install

  1. Tap Download APK above and open the file.
  2. The first time, Android asks whether your browser may install apps → Allow.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you: Install anyway (the app isn't from the Play Store).
  4. Open SubLens, allow Display over other apps, then tap Start translator.

Switch greyed out ("Controlled by restricted setting")? That's Android's protection for apps from outside the Play Store. Unlock it once: Settings → Apps → SubLens → ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, then turn on Display over other apps. Google's guide

Updates come through the app: the button at the bottom turns into New version available – install now? Your settings are kept.

What SubLens does

  • Translation in the lens: every text block is replaced where it is, in the colours of the original.
  • Subtitles, chats, any text on top of YouTube, Telegram, WeChat, websites and games.
  • Reply line: type in your language, tap copy, paste into the chat in theirs.
  • Camera: point it at a menu, sign or poster and read it in your language – live, as a sharp photo, or from your gallery.
  • QR codes: open the link, or Open + translate with the lens running – e.g. to order on a restaurant's website in Chinese.
  • Chat mode and video mode: in chat mode GIFs, animated emojis and profile pictures are ignored, so translations stay still. Tap ▶ on the lens for video mode and subtitles keep up.
  • Preview shows the original until you tap again.
  • Lens you can move and resize, always full width, portrait and landscape, in the colour you like. The reply line goes above or below it.
  • Offline: text recognition and translation run on your phone. No server, no account, nothing is saved.
  • 50+ languages with automatic detection and a swap button, each pack downloads once (about 30 MB). Chinese in traditional or simplified characters.

Good to know

  • Android only: iOS doesn't allow overlays on top of other apps.
  • When the screen asks for sharing, choose Entire screen.
  • When something changes under the lens, only the affected translations hide for a split second to re-read; the rest stays still. Prefer no flicker at all? Show the translation in the bar instead.
  • Netflix, Disney+ and banking apps block screen sharing, so SubLens sees nothing there.
  • Text recognition reads Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari script.
